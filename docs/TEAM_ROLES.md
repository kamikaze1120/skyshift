## Screening questions for each candidate
- What have you actually built, and what part did you own?
- Both days at the event, or partial availability? (Be honest — it changes the plan.)
- In person or remote, and which hours?

## Onboarding checklist (once someone joins)
1. Register on spaceappschallenge.org and join the team page.
2. Add as a GitHub collaborator; add their username to `CODEOWNERS` for their lane.
3. Point them at `README.md` → `docs/CHALLENGE.md` → `docs/JUDGING_CRITERIA.md` → `CONTRIBUTING.md`, in that order.
4. Data/backend role: run IRSA's "Introduction to SPHEREx Spectral Images" tutorial before Oct 28, and answer — can we find an asteroid that appears in two observations with visible movement? (Shapes the whole product.)
